Beyond the Heatmap: can we tell from a medicine's chemistry if it will make you sleepy or keep you awake?

Medicines for mood and mental health work by latching onto tiny "docking spots" in the brain. Two of these spots act like the pedals of a car:

Brake
A spot that slows you down
When a medicine blocks it, you feel drowsy. (scientific name: H1)
Gas
A spot that speeds you up
When a medicine blocks it, you may stay alert. (scientific name: SERT)
Grip
How hard a medicine presses
How tightly it holds onto a spot. Higher = presses harder.
Data: PDSP Ki Database · SIDER 4.1 · PubChem. Built with D3 v7.
